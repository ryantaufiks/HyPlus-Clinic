How it works

From booking to prescription, in four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Book or walk in

    Request a time online and we confirm it on WhatsApp, or simply come to the clinic.

  2. 2

    Check in at the kiosk

    Choose your doctor, step on the scale and measure your blood pressure. Get your queue ticket.

  3. 3

    See your doctor

    Your number appears on the screen. Scan your ticket at the door; your doctor already has your vitals.

  4. 4

    Prescription & records

    Your diagnosis and prescription are saved to your patient portal, ready to print.
