Pair a new device

  1. If the device was used with the OMRON connect app, remove it there and in the phone's Bluetooth settings first. A device can only talk to one host.
  2. Hold the device's Bluetooth button until -P- blinks on its display.
  3. Click Scan, then Pair while -P- is still blinking. The model is detected from the device automatically. The device shows OK when pairing worked.
