Questions

Which devices work with Hy+ Clinic?

Today the OMRON HEM-6232T blood pressure monitor and the OMRON HN-300T2 scale are integrated. Pulse oximeters, thermometers and blood glucose, cholesterol and uric acid meters are next. If your clinic already uses a Bluetooth device, we can look at integrating it.

Can it run under our own brand and domain?

Yes. The name, logo, colours, domain, services and prices are yours, and each clinic gets its own installation and database.

What hardware does a clinic need?

A tablet or touch screen for the kiosk, a TV for the waiting room, the health-check devices and a small computer at the clinic that reads the devices over Bluetooth.

Is patient data sent to the AI?

Only de-identified data: no names, IDs, phone numbers or addresses. The doctor always reviews the summary; it does not diagnose.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the number of sites, devices and the support you need. Contact us and we will send a proposal.
